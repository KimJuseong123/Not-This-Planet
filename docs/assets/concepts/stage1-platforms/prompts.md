## Central assembly dock

Use case: stylized-concept.

Alien plants are broad purple mushroom-tree canopies, indigo leaves and violet flowers; terrain is muted olive moss and rounded gray-brown rock. Match this soft approachable cooperative space exploration mood.
Primary object: a substantial but LOW circular ground-mounted assembly platform in the center of the map where players bring four LARGE spaceship modules and STACK THEM VERTICALLY into a single spaceship. NOT a furniture workbench. ONE central flat solid docking disk, not four separate item slots. Four squat rounded mechanical docking latches evenly spaced around the central disk and placed diagonally between access routes. Four broad shallow ramps at cardinal directions, continuous walkable outer apron. No tall machinery, pillars, overhead gantry, holographic obstruction or railings. Keep central sky and approaches clear for hauling and vertical launch. Body is chunky ivory shell over lavender plinth, lavender recessed central metal disk, concentric subtle cyan alignment ring, inward pointing simple inlaid guide marks. Cyan rectangular inset lamps on perimeter. Tiny warm amber waiting indicators on latch faces; cyan indicates locked. Grounded, mechanically plausible and easy to model with a handful of separate mesh pieces; no exposed cable spaghetti or excessive sci-fi greebles.
Composition: premium art-director presentation board on warm light gray background, beautifully balanced with thin separators and generous whitespace. Large three-quarter EMPTY platform hero view occupies left 60%, set on a very small patch of alien moss with a few violet plants and rounded stones only along far edge, object fully visible, every ramp readable. Right upper 25% contains clean true top view of SAME empty design. Right middle contains clean side elevation of SAME design with short ramp profile and shallow deck. Right bottom small docking-detail vignette shows bottom ivory/lavender engine module physically resting on the center disk with a latch engaged (not levitating). Along bottom left a small separate silhouette inset shows FOUR rounded spaceship modules vertically stacked over the same platform, only to communicate function, subordinate to platform hero. Bottom-to-top module shapes: egg engine body with two circular side pods; flatter toroidal hover module; tall cabin capsule with purple side window; rounded pointed-top tractor cockpit with short antenna. Do not make the central hero occupied.
Short accurate English labels only: "EMPTY DOCK", "TOP", "SIDE", "LOCK DETAIL", "STACK ASSEMBLY". Small material swatches labeled "IVORY", "LAVENDER", "CYAN". No invented numerical dimensions or long text. No characters, logos, hazard stripes, weapons, rusty scrapyard look, gritty realism, medieval altar, saturated neon, floating platform. Soft neutral daylight, gentle ambient occlusion, clear material separation, professional handoff-quality consistent multi-view concept art.
