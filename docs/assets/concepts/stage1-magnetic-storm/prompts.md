Smooth deep navy studio backdrop and minimal slate ground shadow, no buildings, plants, text, labels, grids, collage, panels, UI. Premium production concept painting / stylized 3D volumetric render, dimensional billows, teal highlights and violet shadows, not realistic apocalyptic grey clouds, not flat cartoon. Wide landscape image. This will be the master design for left/right/back/top matching views.
